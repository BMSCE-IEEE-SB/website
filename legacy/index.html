Institutional Heritage & Bangalore Section

Established in 2009 under IEEE Region 10 (Asia-Pacific) and the IEEE Bangalore Section, the BMSCE IEEE Student Branch operates within B.M.S. College of Engineering—one of India's earliest engineering institutions founded in 1946.

Our branch bridges foundational engineering rigor with contemporary industrial research. Through peer-led Special Interest Groups (SIGs), national symposiums, and community outreach, our members consistently publish in IEEE Xplore, secure regional hackathon podiums, and lead technology initiatives across academia.
